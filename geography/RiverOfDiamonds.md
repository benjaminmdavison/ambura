Flowing south out of the [[Borovian Mountains]], the River of Diamonds earns her name by the way the sunlight glimmers on the surface of the water. She flows from the mountains roughly west-southwest, passing by the western borders of [[Brelas’ Shield]] and [[The Screelands]] before defining the southern border of [[The Crossroads]] and later merging into the[[ River Twain]] and flowing to the sea.
