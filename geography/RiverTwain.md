The River Twain is muddy, fast, and surprisingly deep. It runs southwest from the foot of the [[Borovian Mountains]], passing through forests and grasslands before cleaving the [[Free City of Porphyrium]] in two. It then spills into the delta dividing the [[Pearl Sea]] from the [[Western Sea]].
