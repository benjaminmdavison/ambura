The Pearl Sea lies south of the [[Norsatch Promontory]]. 

A relatively shallow sea, it contains many lush tropical islands, including the volcanic island of [[Finch Glen]], which is home to the [[Sea Elves]].  and is filled with pirates, smugglers, and bandits. Pods of whales, ample schools of fish, and beautiful reefs are common features of the Pearl Sea. 

Some mariners whisper of merfolk living in the waters of the Pearl Sea. Superstitious captains will sometimes pay tribute to the merfolk as they sail. 

Apart from the pirates and smugglers, the Pearl Sea is traversed by traders visiting [[the Southlands]]. It eventually deepens into the forbidding [[South Sea]], whose angry waters and high waves have sent many ships plunging into the deep.
