Sticking out of the Southestern corner of Ambura, just below the Kingdom of Breland, is the Norsatch Promontory. 

The promontory is divided from the land by the [[Dragonback Mountains]]. The mountains provide a natural barrier between the promontory and the rest of Ambura, which has historically kept the principality of [[St. Alberich]] - the only settlement on the promontory - safe from invasion. In fact, the geography of the Norsatch Promontory is exquisitely defensible: a rough mountain pass to the north, inaccessible coastlines on the east and west, and a single, easily defended southern harbor make the promontory an ideal place to set up and rule over a smallish kingdom. 

The eastern edge of the promontory is defined by high ridges and rocky cliffs that overlook the [[Peppered Coast]]. 

The western edge of the promontory faces the [[Western Sea]]. Jagged, rocky beaches and heavy waves make it all but impossible to come ashore on the western edge of the promontory. 

The southern tip of the promontory is a large, crater-shaped natural harbor. This harbor has been a boon for St. Alberich: it is easily defensible and provides easy access to commerce and ocean resources. 

South of the promontory is the [[Pearl Sea]], a shallow sea containing plenty of tropical islands, pirates, and smugglers.
