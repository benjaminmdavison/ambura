# Campaign History:

Zoong Pewterleaf, a halfling from Bramblewood, has been sent out on a mysterious errand by Gran: he must go to the mysterious land of St. Alberich to seek his fate. His companion on this journey is Heingveir Rock-Borer, a Dwarven devotee of the creator god Ptah. 

# June 9, 2026
## Endpoint:
It is late evening. Zoong and Heingveir are getting acquainted in the Wriggling Eel in Porphyrium. They are destined for the land of St. Alberich, and can get there via an overland passage or a sea journey. 

Zoong is debating finding work, and is considering checking the following places:
Sorcerors Enclave
Bookgrove
Caravan guard work

## Summary:

Zoong had a farewell dinner with his family and departed Bramblewood with a trade caravan. They encountered wildcats and a windstorm, but made it to Porphyrium intact. Zoong thwarted a pickpocket and made his way to The Wriggling Eel. 

# June 2, 2026
## Endpoint: 
Zoong has learned that his "destiny" lies to the East. He has shopped for equipment and is preparing to take a walk by the pond before preparing a goodbye dinner with his family.
