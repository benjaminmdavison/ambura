Obsidian Mountain is a dark, steep, looming mountain, surrounded by deep clouds, heavy fog, and whipping winds. The peak of the mountain has never been reached. Towards the base of the mountain, about 2,000 feet above sea level, one can find the [[City of Borovia]].
