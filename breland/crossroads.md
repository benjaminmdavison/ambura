Follow the [[River of Diamonds]] west, and you will come to The Crossroads. As the name would suggest, The Crossroads is a trading hub where many roads and routes intersect. Westbound roads from [[Brelas’ Shield ]]and [[The Screelands]] meet eastbound roads from the [[Free City of Porphyrium]] and the north-south road to [[Borovia (Kingdom)]]. With so many routes intersecting in one place, The Crossroads is a dynamic city populated with all manner of beings. 

Overseen by the ambitious [[Bors Wyrmbane the Bold, Lord of the Crossroads]], The Crossroads is growing fast. Bors has sent recruiters out across the world to find traders and migrants to bring back to The Crossroads. The flow of commerce through the city gives Bors enormous power. He is not afraid to use the power of the state to extract taxes and fees from traders and merchants, and has been known to ‘divert’ shipments from uncooperative entities. Tolls, taxes, and fees seem to vary merchant to merchant. Who you know determines how much business you can get done in The Crossroads. It is a good place to have connections, and a bad place to be on the wrong side of the powerful.

## Districts

**The Drag.** Main commercial district. Features massive three-way intersection: roads to Porphyrium, Brelas' Shield, and The Screelands meet in The Drag. There is a triangular 'ring road' around the outside of The Drag where wagons and horses travel. Open-air markets, warehouses full of traders and all manner of goods. 
- The Packing House: fresh meat and produce from The Screelands
- The Crossroad Stables. An enormous stabling-house featuring a blacksmith, barrel-makers, horse-tenders, and a large inn. 

**Riverfront.** This is the district adjacent to the river, just south of The Drag. It features many slips and docks in the River of Diamonds that are equipped to handle small, river-based merchant vessels. 
- Botswain's: an inn and pub, oriented to the sailing folk.
- Riverfront Trader. Enormous pawn shop and general store. 
- The Fish Market. 
- The Government House. Situated squarely between Riverfront and The Drag, the bottom floors are filled with customs and tax agents. The higher floors are used for ceremonial purposes and house the government officers. A large barracks next door is where the city guard is located. 

**The Slash.** A long, narrow, winding neighborhood full of squalid houses, located behind The Drag. This is where many of the less fortunate in the city live. Rumors abound that the Redistributor's Guild is present here. There are a few sketchy, squalid inns and shops.
- The Rats Nest: inn and pub, very cheap. 
- Loony Lukes: fence, shady pawn shop. 
- The Slop House: grub. 

The formal boundary between The Slash and The Drag is a row of warehouses known as **Arson Row**. Due to repeated arsons, these warehouses are made of brick and mortar and feature reinforced back walls. Less fortunate, or poorly connected merchants end up storing goods or operating here.

**The Outskirts.** Small to middle-sized farms of all descriptions dot the landscape east-northeast of the city. There are a few inns and smiths here. 
- Horse Shoe Inn: blacksmith, stable, small attached farm. 

**Charm City.** Just west of The Drag, Charm City features cabarets, ale-houses, and other forms of entertainment. 

**College Row.** A few mages, scholars, and printers set up shop in a small strip of buildings wedged between The Drag, Charm City, and The Slash.
