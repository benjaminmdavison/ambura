The Borovian Mountains are harsh, forbidding, and widely considered to be impassable. Steep slopes, sharp rocks, plunging abysses, and unforgiving weather make these mountains extremely unhospitable to all but the toughest of creatures. The mountains compose the southern border of the Ice Fields.

No man has returned from an adventure into the Ice Fields. 

The most prominent southerly peak of the Borovian Mountains is the [[Obsidian Mountain]], out of which is carved the [[City of Borovia]].
